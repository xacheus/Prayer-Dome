Kingdom Support

How Kingdom Support works

All four programmes follow the same simple path. Choose a programme, choose an approved plan or savings contribution, and the ministry keeps every payment and status visible in your account.

Choose a programme and plan

Pick Business Education, Financial Cover, Savings Programme or Community & Family Support, then the exact approved plan or contribution amount. Every price is shown on the card.

Create a checkout

Sign in and press Choose Plan. The ministry issues a unique payment reference and shows the exact amount, Mobile Money number and payment instructions.

Pay and upload the receipt

Send the exact amount by Mobile Money, then upload the receipt against your reference. Your record waits in “Pending verification”.

Verification and activation

A finance administrator checks the payment against the ministry account. Only then does the plan become active — you can follow every status in My Plans.

Where it goes

Support that reaches real homes

Each programme exists for a practical need in the church family — a child in school, a family business, a household carrying grief, or a member building a savings buffer. These are the four purposes your contributions support.

Learning that lasts

The Education plan builds a 12-month fund for school-related costs such as fees, books, uniforms and exam needs, so a child is not sent home because a term is hard.

Work that provides

The Business plan helps traders, farmers and small business owners protect stock, tools or equipment — keeping a household's income steady through a difficult season.

A community that carries

The Compassion plan stands with families during funerals and other heavy seasons, covering the practical costs that arrive when people are least able to carry them.

Questions

Before you enrol

Straight answers to what members ask most. If your question is not here, the team is one call or email away.

Who can enrol?

Any approved, signed-in member can enrol. Choose a plan or savings contribution, pay its exact amount and upload the receipt. The record appears under My Plans as soon as checkout is created, and a plan becomes active only after the finance team verifies your payment. Savings are credited to your wallet, not activated as a plan.

How much do I need to contribute?

Every plan has one exact monthly price, printed in the plan selector on its card. Education and Business plans start at $5.00 a month and rise to $500.00; Compassion plans start at $2.50 and rise to $50.00. Savings contributions start at $5.00 and are not a subscription. Choose the amount you can sustain for the full programme rather than the largest tier.

How long does a plan run?

A plan contribution is billed monthly and each verified payment covers one month. The support programme matures after 12 months. My Plans shows the current term, activation date, expiry date and programme progress from the dates stored in the database.

Can I hold more than one plan?

You can hold one record per plan, and plans under different programmes can run at the same time. Savings sit alongside them in the Secure Wallet. Every payment reference, proof status and plan status stays visible under My Plans.

Is this insurance or an investment?

No. Kingdom Support is a church mutual-support program, not an insurance policy and not an investment product. It does not promise interest or a financial return. It is a disciplined way for members to prepare for a known need together.

What happens when I need to claim?

Once a plan is active and the programme has matured, submit the claim from My Plans with the information the programme requires. The ministry team reviews it and updates the status. You can follow every change from the same screen.

How is my information protected?

Your account is protected by Firebase Authentication. Payment receipts upload to the ministry's secure Cloudinary storage. Only authorised finance administrators can verify a payment, and only they can approve or reject a claim. Your plan cannot be activated by a browser request.
