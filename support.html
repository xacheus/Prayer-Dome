Kingdom Support

How Kingdom Support works

Education, Business and Compassion plans all follow the same simple path. You choose the tier, contribute what you can sustain, and the ministry keeps every step visible in your account.

Choose a plan

Pick Education, Business or Compassion, then the exact monthly plan that matches your budget. Every price is shown above.

Create a checkout

Sign in and press Subscribe. The ministry issues a unique payment reference and shows the exact amount and payment instructions.

Pay and upload the receipt

Send the exact amount by Mobile Money, then upload the receipt against your reference. Your plan waits in “Awaiting verification”.

Verification and activation

A finance administrator checks the payment against the ministry account. Only then does the plan become active — you can follow every status in My Plans.

Where it goes

Support that reaches real homes

Every program exists for a practical need in the church family — a child in school, a family business, or a household carrying grief. These are the three purposes your plan supports.

Learning that lasts

The Education plan builds a 12-month fund for school-related costs such as fees, books, uniforms and exam needs, so a child is not sent home because a term is hard.

Work that provides

The Business plan helps traders, farmers and small business owners protect stock, tools or equipment — keeping a household's income steady through a difficult season.

A community that carries

The Compassion plan stands with families during funerals and other heavy seasons, covering the practical costs that arrive when people are least able to carry them.

Questions

Before you enrol

Straight answers to what members ask most. If your question is not here, the team is one call or email away.

Who can enrol?

Any approved, signed-in member can subscribe. Choose a plan, pay its exact monthly amount and upload the receipt. The plan appears under My Plans as soon as checkout is created, and becomes active only after the finance team verifies your payment.

How much do I need to contribute?

Every plan has one exact monthly price, printed on its plan card above. Education and Business start at $5.00 a month and rise to $500.00; Compassion starts at $2.50 and rises to $50.00. Choose the plan you can sustain for the full 12-month programme rather than the largest tier.

How long does a plan run?

Your subscription is billed monthly and each verified payment covers one month. The support programme matures after 12 months. My Plans shows the current term, the renewal date and the programme progress from the dates stored in the database.

Can I hold more than one plan?

You can hold one subscription per plan, and different plans for Education, Business and Compassion can run at the same time. Every payment reference and every plan status stays visible under My Plans.

Is this insurance or an investment?

No. Kingdom Support is a church mutual-support program, not an insurance policy and not an investment product. It does not promise interest or a financial return. It is a disciplined way for members to prepare for a known need together.

What happens when I need to claim?

Once a plan is active and the programme has matured, submit the claim from My Plans with the information the programme requires. The ministry team reviews it and updates the status. You can follow every change from the same screen.

How is my information protected?

Your account is protected by Firebase Authentication. Payment receipts upload to the ministry's secure Cloudinary storage. Only authorised finance administrators can verify a payment, and only they can approve or reject a claim. Your plan cannot be activated by a browser request.
