Prayer Dome official signature mark (S.K. Masuku)
=================================================

The letterforms in pd-signature.svg / pd-signature.png were converted to
outlines from the "Great Vibes" typeface by TypeSETit / the Great Vibes Pro
Project Authors (https://github.com/googlefonts/great-vibes).

Great Vibes is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.
The full licence is available at:
  https://openfontlicense.org/
  https://github.com/googlefonts/great-vibes/blob/main/OFL.txt

The resulting vector artwork is the signing mark used on Prayer Dome
certificates and membership cards. It is not a substitute for a handwritten
signature on a legal instrument.
